Your self-guided introduction to Survival French.

🎯 What is Survival French?

The goal is simple: activate your French. Start speaking, even imperfectly. Notice the language around you. Memorise key phrases to communicate in Brussels — in transport, shops, and with your host family.

📅 This week's challenge

For one week, keep your eyes and ears open. Collect French words from real life — signs, announcements, conversations, your host family.

🗺️ How to use this guide

Go through each section using the tabs above. Watch the videos, complete the activities, and take the quizzes. No rush — go at your own pace.

City Observation 🚇
Pay attention to the announcements in public transport — they are your French classroom this week!

🚉 Metro, Tram & Bus stations

Memorise the names of 3 stations near where you live or commute. Listen to the announcements and try to repeat them. e.g. Montgomery

🚌 Transport lines

Memorise 3 line numbers you use regularly — metro, tram, or bus. Listen when they are announced and try to say the number in French.

Your Host Family 🏠
Your host family is your best French resource. Don't be shy — they want to help!

💬 Your word of the week

Listen carefully at home. Memorise 1 phrase or 1 word you hear regularly — or ask your host family: "What is your favourite word in French?"

Write it down — practice saying it aloud!

🗣️ Try these with your host family

  • Bonjour ! — Say good morning in French every day this week
  • Merci beaucoup ! — Thank them for meals, help, anything
  • C'est délicieux ! — Say it about their cooking (only if you mean it)
  • Comment on dit... ? — Ask "how do you say...?"
